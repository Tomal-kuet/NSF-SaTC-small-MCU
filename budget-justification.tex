\section*{Budget Justification}
\noindent University of Texas at El Paso

\subsection*{A. Senior Personnel}
\textbf{Principal Investigator -- MD Armanuzzaman} requests 1 calendar month of effort in each of the three project years (3 calendar months over the life of the award).
The PI will provide overall research leadership for the project, directing Thrust~1 (Application-Specific Trusted Execution Domains, on reconfigurable FPGA hardware and ARM's
Confidential Compute Architecture), Thrust~2 (Scalable Runtime Trust Attestation, on Cortex-M bare-metal and RTOS platforms), and Thrust~3 (Evidence-Guided Trust Containment and Recovery), mentor and supervise the graduate research assistant, manage reporting requirements, and disseminate project results through peer-reviewed publications and conference presentations.
Annual salary requests are \$12,807 (Year 1), \$13,191 (Year 2), and \$13,587 (Year 3).
\textbf{Total (3 years, wages only): \$39,585.}

\subsection*{B. Other Personnel}
\textbf{Graduate Research Assistant:} One Ph.D. student in the T\textsubscript{3}SLab is
budgeted at 0.5 FTE (50\% time, the standard academic-year research-assistant appointment)
for each of the three project years, supporting implementation and evaluation across all three
thrusts: trusted-domain composition and attestation on FPGA and ARM~CCA platforms (Thrust~1),
scalable runtime evidence generation on Cortex-M (Thrust~2), and compartmentalization and
evidence-guided recovery (Thrust~3). Annual stipends are \$30,824 (Year 1), \$31,749 (Year 2), and \$32,701 (Year 3).
\textbf{Total (3 years, wages only): \$95,274.}

\subsection*{C. Fringe Benefits}
Fringe benefits are calculated using UTEP's current rates:
\begin{itemize}
	\item Faculty: premium monthly rate of \$878.69 and benefits rate of 17.03\%.
	\item Graduate students: premium monthly rate of \$286.50 and benefits rate of 0.88\%.
\end{itemize}
Applying these rates over the PI's 1 calendar month and the Ph.D. student's stipend in each
project year gives combined fringe of \$6,769 (Year 1), \$6,842 (Year 2), and \$6,918 (Year 3).
\textbf{Total Fringe Benefits (3 years): \$20,529.}

\subsection*{D. Equipment}
\textbf{Total: \$12,000} (\$6,000 Year 1, \$6,000 Year 2, \$0 Year 3). Two single items each exceed UTEP's
\$5,000 equipment capitalization threshold and are budgeted here rather than under Materials
and Supplies:
\begin{itemize}
	\item \textbf{Year 1 -- Arm Juno R2 development board}, \$6,000, for Thrust~1's Root-monitor
	and world-switching firmware.
	No commercially available development board currently exposes ARM's Realm Management
	Extension (RME), so Thrust~1's ARM CCA work is otherwise evaluated only on the ARM Fixed
	Virtual Platform (software simulation); Juno gives real Cortex-A hardware to validate the
	Root-monitor and secure-world firmware logic outside simulation, following the same
	FVP-plus-Juno methodology used in prior CCA-adjacent work (e.g., Shelter, cited in Thrust~1's
	Related Work). We will migrate to RME-capable silicon in place of Juno should it become
	commercially available during the award period.
	\item \textbf{Year 2 -- Arm MPS3 Cortex prototyping system} (V2M-MPS3-0341A, Kintex
	UltraScale FPGA), \$6,000, for the attestation and compartmentalization prototypes
	(Thrusts~2--3). It provides an FPGA-hosted Cortex-M85 profile with TrustZone-M and Pointer
	Authentication, consistent with the platform used in the PI's preliminary ENOLA results.

\end{itemize}

\subsection*{E. Travel}
A total of \$5,000 per year (domestic only) is budgeted to support travel for the PI and the
graduate research assistant to attend top-tier security conferences, including but not limited
to IEEE S\&P, ACM CCS, USENIX Security, NDSS, and ACSAC, consistent with UTEP and NSF
travel policies. Costs include airfare, registration fees, lodging, per diem (meals), and
ground transportation. \textbf{Total (3 years): \$15,000.}

\subsection*{F. Participant Support Costs}
\$0.

\subsection*{G. Other Direct Costs}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.,leftmargin=1.6em]
	\item \textbf{Materials and Supplies:} \$5,000 (Year 1), \$0 (Year 2), \$0 (Year 3). Year 1
	funds smaller testbed hardware and consumables to complement the Section~D equipment (\textbf{Total: \$5,000}):
	\begin{itemize}
		\item Peripherals and consumables (power supplies, cabling, connectors, and spare
		parts) to support the Section~D testbed boards across all three years -- \$1,520.
		\item Two Radxa ROCK~5B (RK3588) single-board computers for OpenCCA-based software
		emulation of ARM CCA/Realm semantics on real, non-RME Cortex-A76/A55 hardware,
		complementing the FVP and Juno~R2 platforms budgeted in Section~D (Thrust~1)
		-- \$900.
		\item JTAG/SWD debug probes and a USB logic analyzer for firmware bring-up and
		precise timing measurement of domain-switch and MPU-transition overhead across the
		Cortex-A and Cortex-M testbed boards (Thrusts~1 and~3) -- \$1,580.
		\item Storage media, cabling, and networking accessories to complete the
		ROCK~5B, MPS3, and Juno testbeds -- \$1,000.
	\end{itemize}
	
	\item \textbf{Publication Costs:} \$0 (Year 1), \$2,500 (Year 2), \$2,500 (Year 3), to
	cover open-access publication and conference proceedings fees as results become
	available. \textbf{Total: \$5,000.}
	\item \textbf{Consultant Services:} None.
	\item \textbf{Computer Services:} None.
	\item \textbf{Subawards:} None.
	\item \textbf{Other (Tuition):} Ph.D. student tuition remission of \$6,902 per year is
	included for three years, totaling \textbf{\$20,706}. Tuition is excluded from the
	indirect cost base in accordance with NSF and UTEP policy.
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Total Other Direct Costs (3 years): \$30,706.}

\subsection*{H. Total Direct Costs}
Year 1: \$73,302, Year 2: \$72,184, Year 3: \$67,608. \textbf{Total (3 years): \$213,094.}

\subsection*{I. Indirect Costs}
Indirect costs are calculated at UTEP's federally negotiated rate of 53.5\% of Modified Total
Direct Costs (MTDC). MTDC excludes tuition and equipment, in addition to other unallowable
categories, giving an MTDC base of \$60,400 (Year 1), \$59,282 (Year 2), and \$60,706 (Year 3).
\textbf{Total Indirect Costs (3 years): \$96,508.}
