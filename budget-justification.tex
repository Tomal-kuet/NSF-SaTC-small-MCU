\section*{Budget Justification}
\vspace{-0.3cm}
University of Texas at El Paso

\subsection*{A. Senior Personnel}
\textbf{Principal Investigator -- MD Armanuzzaman (UTEP)} requests 1.5 calendar months of
effort in each of the three project years (4.5 calendar months over the life of the award). The
PI will provide overall research leadership for the project, direct the development of
verifiable trust establishment techniques for embedded and microcontroller-class systems
(control-flow attestation, hardware-enabled trust, and FPGA/SoC security), mentor and
supervise the graduate research assistant, manage reporting requirements, and disseminate
project results through peer-reviewed publications and conference presentations.
\textbf{Total (3 years): \$61,157.}

\subsection*{B. Other Personnel}
\textbf{Graduate Research Assistant:} One graduate student in the T\textsubscript{3}SLab is
budgeted for each of the three project years, supporting research on embedded systems
security, hardware-enabled trust, and FPGA/SoC security. \arman{Confirm FTE\% and
Ph.D./M.S. level -- the Summary Proposal Budget lists one graduate student per year with a
Ph.D.-level tuition remission (Section G.6 below), so this is assumed to be the Ph.D.
student.} Annual stipends are \$25,864 (Year 1), \$26,640 (Year 2), and \$27,440 (Year 3).
\textbf{Total (3 years): \$79,944.}

\subsection*{C. Fringe Benefits}
Fringe benefits are calculated using UTEP's federally negotiated composite fringe benefit
rates for faculty and graduate research assistants: \$8,354 (Year 1), \$8,461 (Year 2), and
\$8,572 (Year 3). \textbf{Total Fringe Benefits (3 years): \$25,387.}

\subsection*{D. Equipment}
\$0. No single item of equipment exceeding \$10,000 is requested; smaller embedded-security
evaluation hardware is budgeted under Materials and Supplies (Section G.1).

\subsection*{E. Travel}
A total of \$5,000 per year (domestic only) is budgeted to support travel for the PI and the
graduate research assistant to attend top-tier security conferences, including but not limited
to IEEE S\&P, ACM CCS, USENIX Security, NDSS, and ACSAC, consistent with UTEP and NSF
travel policies. \textbf{Total (3 years): \$15,000.}

\subsection*{F. Participant Support Costs}
\$0.

\subsection*{G. Other Direct Costs}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.,leftmargin=1.6em]
	\item \textbf{Materials and Supplies:} \$10,000 (Year 1), \$0 (Year 2), \$0 (Year 3).
	Year 1 funds support one-time acquisition of embedded-security evaluation hardware and
	lab consumables (e.g., microcontroller/FPGA evaluation boards, side-channel evaluation
	tooling, and development boards). \arman{Confirm/expand itemized list if research.gov
	requires per-item breakdown.} \textbf{Total: \$10,000.}
	\item \textbf{Publication Costs:} \$0 (Year 1), \$2,500 (Year 2), \$2,500 (Year 3), to
	cover open-access publication and conference proceedings fees as results become
	available. \textbf{Total: \$5,000.}
	\item \textbf{Consultant Services:} None.
	\item \textbf{Computer Services:} None.
	\item \textbf{Subawards:} None.
	\item \textbf{Other (Tuition):} Graduate student tuition remission of \$6,902 per year is
	included for three years, totaling \textbf{\$20,706}. Tuition is excluded from the
	indirect cost base in accordance with NSF and UTEP policy.
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Total Other Direct Costs (3 years): \$35,706.}

\subsection*{H. Total Direct Costs}
Year 1: \$75,906, Year 2: \$69,883, Year 3: \$71,405. \textbf{Total (3 years): \$217,194.}

\subsection*{I. Indirect Costs}
Indirect costs are calculated at UTEP's federally negotiated rate of 53.5\% of Modified Total
Direct Costs (MTDC). MTDC excludes tuition and other unallowable categories: \$69,004
(Year 1), \$62,981 (Year 2), and \$64,503 (Year 3). \textbf{Total Indirect Costs (3 years):
\$105,121.}
